Лемм 11.12 Дирихлегийн цөм.

\begin{equation*} D_n(t) = \frac12 + \sum_{k=1}^n\cos kt = \frac{\sin(n+\frac12)t}{2\sin\frac t2} \quad (t\ne2\pi m), \qquad \frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}D_n(t)\,dt = 1, \qquad \frac1\pi\int_0^\pi D_n(t)\,dt = \frac12. \end{equation*}

Баталгаа.
\(2\sin\frac t2\cos kt = \sin(k+\frac12)t - \sin(k-\frac12)t\text{;}\) \(k = 1..n\)-ээр нийлбэрлэвэл телескоп: \(2\sin\frac t2\sum\cos kt = \sin(n+\frac12)t - \sin\frac t2\text{.}\) Интегралууд нь \(\int_{-\pi}^\pi\cos kt = 0\)-ээс, \(D_n\) тэгш тул.
