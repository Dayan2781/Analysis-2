Теорем 12.28 Фруллани.

\(f\) нь \([0,\infty)\) дээр тасралтгүй, \(\lim_{x\to+\infty}f(x) = f(\infty)\) төгсгөлөг бол \(a,b>0\) үед
\begin{equation*} \int_0^\infty\frac{f(ax) - f(bx)}{x}\,dx = \bigl(f(0) - f(\infty)\bigr)\ln\frac ba. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Баталгаа.
\(0<\varepsilon<R\) үед орлуулгаар
\begin{equation*} \int_\varepsilon^R\frac{f(ax) - f(bx)}{x}dx = \int_{a\varepsilon}^{aR}\frac{f(t)}{t}dt - \int_{b\varepsilon}^{bR}\frac{f(t)}{t}dt = \int_{a\varepsilon}^{b\varepsilon}\frac{f(t)}{t}dt - \int_{aR}^{bR}\frac{f(t)}{t}dt. \end{equation*}
Дундаж утгын теоремоор \(\int_{a\varepsilon}^{b\varepsilon}\frac{f(t)}{t}dt = f(\xi_\varepsilon)\ln\frac ba\text{,}\) \(\xi_\varepsilon\) нь \(a\varepsilon\text{,}\) \(b\varepsilon\)-ийн хооронд, иймд \(\to f(0)\ln\frac ba\text{.}\) Адилаар \(\int_{aR}^{bR}\frac{f(t)}{t}dt = f(\eta_R)\ln\frac ba\to f(\infty)\ln\frac ba\text{.}\)
