Лемм 11.2 Ортогональ чанар.

\(m, n\in\{0,1,2,\dots\}\) бол
\begin{equation*} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\cos mx\cos nx\,dx = \int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\sin mx\sin nx\,dx = \begin{cases}0, & m\ne n,\\ \pi, & m = n\ge1,\end{cases} \qquad \int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\cos mx\sin nx\,dx = 0, \end{equation*}
мөн \(\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}1\,dx = 2\pi\text{.}\)

Баталгаа.
\(\cos mx\cos nx = \frac12[\cos(m-n)x + \cos(m+n)x]\) ба \(k\ne0\) үед \(\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\cos kx\,dx = 0\text{.}\) \(m = n\ge1\) үед \(\frac12\int(1 + \cos2nx) = \pi\text{.}\) Бусад нь адил; \(\cos mx\sin nx\) нь сондгой функц.
