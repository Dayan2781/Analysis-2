Жишээ 8.4 Үндсэн жишээ.

\begin{equation*} \int_1^{+\infty} \frac{dx}{x^p} \ \text{нийлнэ} \iff p > 1, \qquad \text{энэ үед}\ \int_1^{+\infty}\frac{dx}{x^p} = \frac{1}{p-1}. \end{equation*}
\(p \ne 1\) үед \(\int_1^b x^{-p}\,dx = \frac{b^{1-p} - 1}{1-p}\text{.}\) \(b \to +\infty\) үед \(p > 1\) бол \(b^{1-p} \to 0\text{,}\) \(p < 1\) бол \(b^{1-p} \to +\infty\text{.}\) \(p = 1\) үед \(\int_1^b \frac{dx}{x} = \ln b \to +\infty\text{.}\)
