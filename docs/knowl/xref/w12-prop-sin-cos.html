Өгүүлбэр 12.45.

\(a,b>-1\) үед
\begin{equation*} \int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^a\varphi\cos^b\varphi\,d\varphi = \frac12B\Bigl(\frac{a+1}{2},\frac{b+1}{2}\Bigr) = \frac{\Gamma\bigl(\frac{a+1}{2}\bigr)\Gamma\bigl(\frac{b+1}{2}\bigr)}{2\,\Gamma\bigl(\frac{a+b}{2}+1\bigr)}. \end{equation*}
